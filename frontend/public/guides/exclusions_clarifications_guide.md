## General Exclusions

### Medical Exclusions

Cosmetic and aesthetic procedures are excluded, including plastic surgery for appearance enhancement, laser treatments for non-medical skin conditions, hair transplants and hair loss treatments, and liposuction and body contouring. Experimental or investigational treatments not approved by relevant health authorities are also excluded.

Fertility treatments, IVF, and assisted reproduction procedures are not covered. Organ transplants and related donor costs are excluded. Weight management programs, bariatric surgery, and obesity treatments are excluded unless medically critical.

Policy references: CL-101, CL-102, CL-103, CL-104, CL-105

### Dental Exclusions

Orthodontic treatment for aesthetic purposes, teeth whitening, and cosmetic veneers are excluded for all plans. Dental implants are excluded except for trauma-related cases with incident documentation. Replacement of lost or stolen dentures is not covered.

Policy references: CL-106, CL-107, CL-108, CL-109

### Mental Health Exclusions

Court-mandated therapy or counseling is excluded for all plans. Relationship or marital counseling unrelated to a diagnosed mental health condition is not covered. Treatment for substance abuse, addiction, or rehabilitation is excluded (covered under a separate program).

Policy references: CL-110, CL-111, CL-112

### General Service Exclusions

Services obtained outside Singapore without prior travel approval are excluded. Services provided by immediate family members (spouse, parents, siblings, children) are not covered. Charges for no-show appointments or administrative fees are excluded.

Wellness programs, health screenings, and vaccinations are excluded from medical claims (these are covered under a separate Wellness Budget). Traditional, complementary, or alternative medicine are excluded unless specifically listed as covered.

Policy references: CL-113, CL-114, CL-115, CL-116, CL-117

### Condition-Based Exclusions

Self-inflicted injuries or injuries sustained during illegal activities are excluded. Injuries from extreme sports or hazardous recreational activities are excluded without supplemental coverage. Services for pre-existing conditions during waiting periods are also excluded.

The exclusions list is reviewed annually and employees are notified 30 days before any policy changes take effect. Special exemptions may be granted on a case-by-case basis with medical committee review.

Policy references: CL-118, CL-119, CL-120, CL-121, CL-122

## Outpatient Medical Exclusions

Cosmetic procedures, plastic surgery, and aesthetic treatments are not covered unless medically necessary with specialist certification. Experimental or investigational treatments not approved by HSA (Health Sciences Authority) are excluded. Pre-existing conditions diagnosed before employment start date are excluded for the first 12 months of coverage. Alternative medicine (e.g., traditional Chinese medicine, homeopathy) is excluded unless explicitly covered under a plan addendum.

Policy references: CL-012, CL-013, CL-014, CL-015

---

## Dental Exclusions

Orthodontics (braces, aligners) and cosmetic dentistry (whitening, veneers) are excluded for all plans. Dental implants are excluded unless required due to a workplace accident with an incident report. Services for conditions arising before the waiting period (1 month) are excluded.

Policy references: CL-037, CL-038, CL-039, CL-040

---

## Mental Health Exclusions

Court-ordered counseling or therapy is excluded for all plans. Marriage or couples counseling is excluded unless directly related to a diagnosed mental health condition. Services for substance abuse or addiction are excluded (covered under a separate Wellness Program). Group therapy sessions are excluded; only individual sessions are claimable.

Policy references: CL-059, CL-060, CL-061, CL-062

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is TCM (Traditional Chinese Medicine) covered?**
No, unless explicitly added to your plan via an optional rider.

**Are vaccinations covered?**
Routine vaccinations are covered under the separate Wellness Budget, not medical claims.

**What about mental health for my spouse?**
Dependents aged 12+ are covered under Premium plans with separate session limits.

**Can I claim for services at non-panel clinics?**
Yes, but reimbursement is at 70% of panel rates. Dental services must be at panel clinics.

**I'm going on an overseas assignment. Is my coverage valid?**
Overseas treatment requires prior travel approval. Submit claims within 60 days.

Policy references: CL-149, CL-150, CL-151, CL-147, CL-154
