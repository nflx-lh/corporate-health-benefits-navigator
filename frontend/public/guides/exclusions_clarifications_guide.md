## General Exclusions & Non-Covered Services

### 1. Medical Exclusions
_Provenance: Source file: `exclusions_general.md` | Clause IDs: `CL-101`, `CL-102`, `CL-103`, `CL-104`, `CL-105`_

**CL-101:** Cosmetic and aesthetic procedures are excluded including but not limited to:
- Plastic surgery for appearance enhancement
- Laser treatments for non-medical skin conditions
- Hair transplants and hair loss treatments
- Liposuction and body contouring

**CL-102:** Experimental or investigational treatments not approved by relevant health authorities are excluded.

**CL-103:** Fertility treatments, IVF, and assisted reproduction procedures are excluded.

**CL-104:** Organ transplants and related donor costs are excluded.

**CL-105:** Weight management programs, bariatric surgery, and obesity treatments are excluded unless medically critical.

### 2. Dental Exclusions
_Provenance: Source file: `exclusions_general.md` | Clause IDs: `CL-106`, `CL-107`, `CL-108`, `CL-109`_

**CL-106:** Orthodontic treatment for aesthetic purposes is excluded for all plans.

**CL-107:** Dental implants are excluded except for trauma-related cases with incident documentation.

**CL-108:** Teeth whitening and cosmetic veneers are excluded for all plans.

**CL-109:** Replacement of lost or stolen dentures is excluded.

### 3. Mental Health Exclusions
_Provenance: Source file: `exclusions_general.md` | Clause IDs: `CL-110`, `CL-111`, `CL-112`_

**CL-110:** Court-mandated therapy or counseling is excluded for all plans.

**CL-111:** Relationship or marital counseling unrelated to diagnosed mental health conditions is excluded.

**CL-112:** Treatment for substance abuse, addiction, or rehabilitation is excluded (covered under separate program).

### 4. General Service Exclusions
_Provenance: Source file: `exclusions_general.md` | Clause IDs: `CL-113`, `CL-114`, `CL-115`, `CL-116`, `CL-117`_

**CL-113:** Services obtained outside Singapore without prior travel approval are excluded.

**CL-114:** Services provided by immediate family members (spouse, parents, siblings, children) are excluded.

**CL-115:** Charges for no-show appointments or administrative fees are excluded.

**CL-116:** Wellness programs, health screenings, and vaccinations are excluded (covered under separate Wellness Budget).

**CL-117:** Traditional, complementary, or alternative medicine are excluded unless specifically listed as covered.

### 5. Condition-Based Exclusions
_Provenance: Source file: `exclusions_general.md` | Clause IDs: `CL-118`, `CL-119`, `CL-120`_

**CL-118:** Self-inflicted injuries or injuries sustained during illegal activities are excluded.

**CL-119:** Injuries from extreme sports or hazardous recreational activities are excluded without supplemental coverage.

**CL-120:** Services for pre-existing conditions during waiting periods are excluded.

### Notes
_Provenance: Source file: `exclusions_general.md` | Clause IDs: `CL-121`, `CL-122`_

**CL-121:** Exclusions list reviewed annually; employees notified 30 days before policy changes take effect.

**CL-122:** Special exemptions may be granted on case-by-case basis with medical committee review.

## Outpatient Medical Benefits Policy

### 4. Exclusions
_Provenance: Source file: `outpatient_policy.md` | Clause IDs: `CL-012`, `CL-013`, `CL-014`, `CL-015`_

**CL-012:** Cosmetic procedures, plastic surgery, and aesthetic treatments are not covered unless medically necessary with specialist certification.

**CL-013:** Experimental or investigational treatments not approved by HSA (Health Sciences Authority) are excluded.

**CL-014:** Pre-existing conditions diagnosed before employment start date are excluded for the first 12 months of coverage.

**CL-015:** Alternative medicine (e.g., traditional Chinese medicine, homeopathy) is excluded unless explicitly covered under plan addendum.

## Dental Benefits Policy

### 4. Exclusions
_Provenance: Source file: `dental_policy.md` | Clause IDs: `CL-037`, `CL-038`, `CL-039`, `CL-040`_

**CL-037:** Orthodontics (braces, aligners) are excluded for all plans.

**CL-038:** Cosmetic dentistry (whitening, veneers) is excluded for all plans.

**CL-039:** Dental implants are excluded unless required due to workplace accident with incident report.

**CL-040:** Services for conditions arising before the waiting period (1 month) are excluded.

### 4. Exclusions
_Provenance: Source file: `mental_health_policy.md` | Clause IDs: `CL-059`, `CL-060`, `CL-061`, `CL-062`_

**CL-059:** Court-ordered counseling or therapy is excluded for all plans.

## FAQ Clarifications

### 3. Coverage Scope
_Provenance: Source file: `faq_policy_clarifications.md` | Clause IDs: `CL-149`, `CL-150`, `CL-151`_

**CL-149:** *Q: Is TCM (Traditional Chinese Medicine) covered?*  
A: No, unless explicitly added to your plan via optional rider (see CL-015).

**CL-150:** *Q: Are vaccinations covered?*  
A: Routine vaccinations are covered under the separate Wellness Budget, not medical claims (CL-116).

**CL-151:** *Q: What about mental health for my spouse?*  
A: Dependents aged 12+ are covered under Premium plans with separate session limits (CL-051).

### 2. Claims Questions
_Provenance: Source file: `faq_policy_clarifications.md` | Clause IDs: `CL-147`_

**CL-147:** *Q: Can I claim for services at non-panel clinics?*  
A: Yes, but reimbursement is at 70% of panel rates (CL-025). Dental services must be at panel clinics (CL-032).

### 5. Special Situations
_Provenance: Source file: `faq_policy_clarifications.md` | Clause IDs: `CL-154`_

**CL-154:** *Q: I'm going on overseas assignment. Is my coverage valid?*  
A: Overseas treatment requires prior travel approval. Submit claims within 60 days (CL-093, CL-113).
