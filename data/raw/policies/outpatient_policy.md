# Outpatient Medical Benefits Policy

**Policy ID:** OP-2024-001  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-11-15  
**Owner:** HR Benefits Team

---

## 1. Eligibility

**CL-001:** All active full-time employees on Basic, Plus, or Premium plans are eligible for outpatient medical benefits from their first day of employment.

**CL-002:** Contract employees on Plus or Premium plans are eligible after completing 3 months of continuous service.

**CL-003:** Dependents (spouse and children under 21) are covered under Plus and Premium plans only.

---

## 2. Coverage Rules

**CL-004:** Outpatient consultations at panel clinics and hospitals are covered at varying percentages based on plan tier.

**CL-005:** Basic plan: 60% co-insurance after SGD 20 co-pay per visit.

**CL-006:** Plus plan: 80% co-insurance after SGD 15 co-pay per visit.

**CL-007:** Premium plan: 90% co-insurance after SGD 10 co-pay per visit.

**CL-008:** Specialist consultations require GP referral letter except for Premium plan holders.

---

## 3. Limits & Co-pay

**CL-009:** Annual limits per employee:
- Basic: SGD 1,500
- Plus: SGD 3,000
- Premium: SGD 5,000

**CL-010:** Per-visit co-pay is non-waivable and paid directly to provider.

**CL-011:** Dependents share the same annual limit pool as the primary employee.

---

## 4. Exclusions

**CL-012:** Cosmetic procedures, plastic surgery, and aesthetic treatments are not covered unless medically necessary with specialist certification.

**CL-013:** Experimental or investigational treatments not approved by HSA (Health Sciences Authority) are excluded.

**CL-014:** Pre-existing conditions diagnosed before employment start date are excluded for the first 12 months of coverage.

**CL-015:** Alternative medicine (e.g., traditional Chinese medicine, homeopathy) is excluded unless explicitly covered under plan addendum.

---

## 5. Required Documents

**CL-016:** For standard GP visits:
- Original itemized receipt
- Medical certificate (MC) if claiming sick leave concurrently

**CL-017:** For specialist visits:
- Referral letter from GP
- Specialist consultation report
- Itemized hospital/clinic bill

**CL-018:** For prescription medications:
- Pharmacy receipt with medication names and dosages
- Doctor's prescription slip

---

## 6. Claim Timeline

**CL-019:** Claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days from date of service.

**CL-020:** Late claims may be rejected unless accompanied by written justification approved by HR.

**CL-021:** Reimbursement processing takes 7-10 business days from submission of complete documentation.

---

## 7. Pre-authorization Requirements

**CL-022:** Procedures exceeding SGD 500 per visit require pre-authorization for non-emergency cases, unless a more specific service-level pre-authorization rule applies (see CL-073A for precedence rules and CL-074 for service-specific requirements).

**CL-023:** Emergency room visits are exempt from pre-authorization but require retrospective notification within 48 hours.

---

## Notes

**CL-024:** Panel clinic lists are updated quarterly and available on the employee portal.

**CL-025:** Non-panel providers are reimbursed at 70% of panel rates.
