# Dental Benefits Policy

**Policy ID:** DN-2024-001  
**Version:** 1.8  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-10-20  
**Owner:** HR Benefits Team

---

## 1. Eligibility

**CL-026:** Full-time employees on Plus and Premium plans are eligible for dental benefits after 1 month of service.

**CL-027:** Basic plan holders do not have dental coverage.

**CL-028:** Contract employees are not eligible for dental benefits regardless of plan tier.

**CL-029:** Dependents (spouse and children under 21) are covered under Premium plans only, with separate annual limits.

---

## 2. Coverage Rules

**CL-030:** Plus plan covers:
- Preventive: 80% (scaling, polishing, routine exams)
- Restorative: 60% (fillings, extractions)
- Major: Not covered

**CL-031:** Premium plan covers:
- Preventive: 100%
- Restorative: 80%
- Major: 50% (crowns, bridges, root canals)

**CL-032:** All dental procedures must be performed at panel dental clinics.

---

## 3. Limits & Co-pay

**CL-033:** Annual limits per employee:
- Plus: SGD 800 (preventive + restorative combined)
- Premium: SGD 2,000 (all categories combined)

**CL-034:** Co-pay for Plus plan: SGD 25 per visit.

**CL-035:** Co-pay for Premium plan: SGD 15 per visit.

**CL-036:** Dependent limits (Premium only): SGD 1,200 per dependent per year.

---

## 4. Exclusions

**CL-037:** Orthodontics (braces, aligners) are excluded for all plans.

**CL-038:** Cosmetic dentistry (whitening, veneers) is excluded for all plans.

**CL-039:** Dental implants are excluded unless required due to workplace accident with incident report.

**CL-040:** Services for conditions arising before the waiting period (1 month) are excluded.

---

## 5. Required Documents

**CL-041:** For all claims:
- Itemized dental clinic invoice
- Treatment summary from dentist
- Pre-authorization form (if applicable)

**CL-042:** For major procedures:
- X-ray reports
- Dentist's treatment plan and justification

---

## 6. Claim Timeline

**CL-043:** Submit claims within 30 days of treatment completion.

**CL-044:** Pre-authorization required for any single procedure exceeding SGD 300.

**CL-045:** Reimbursement within 10 business days of approved submission.

---

## Notes

**CL-046:** Panel dental clinics provide direct billing for preventive services on Premium plans.

**CL-047:** Emergency dental services (e.g., trauma) may be claimed retrospectively without pre-auth.
