# Mental Health & Counseling Benefits Policy

**Policy ID:** MH-2024-001  
**Version:** 1.5  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-09-30  
**Owner:** HR Benefits Team

---

## 1. Eligibility

**CL-048:** All active full-time employees on Plus and Premium plans are eligible for mental health benefits after 6 months of continuous service.

**CL-049:** Basic plan holders may access Employee Assistance Program (EAP) only (3 free sessions per year, non-claimable).

**CL-050:** Contract employees on Premium plans are eligible after 12 months of service.

**CL-051:** Dependents (spouse and children aged 12+) are covered under Premium plans with separate session limits.

---

## 2. Coverage Rules

**CL-052:** Plus plan: 70% co-insurance for up to 12 sessions per calendar year.

**CL-053:** Premium plan: 85% co-insurance for up to 20 sessions per calendar year.

**CL-054:** Sessions must be conducted by licensed psychologists, psychiatrists, or accredited counselors on the panel provider list.

**CL-055:** Both in-person and telehealth sessions are covered at the same rate.

---

## 3. Limits & Co-pay

**CL-056:** Annual limits per employee:
- Plus: SGD 1,800 (up to 12 sessions)
- Premium: SGD 3,500 (up to 20 sessions)

**CL-057:** Co-pay: SGD 30 per session for all eligible plans.

**CL-058:** Medication prescribed by psychiatrists is covered under outpatient medical benefits, not mental health category.

---

## 4. Exclusions

**CL-059:** Court-ordered counseling or therapy is excluded for all plans.

**CL-060:** Marriage or couples counseling is excluded unless directly related to diagnosed mental health condition.

**CL-061:** Services for substance abuse or addiction are excluded (covered under separate Wellness Program).

**CL-062:** Group therapy sessions are excluded; only individual sessions are claimable.

---

## 5. Required Documents

**CL-063:** For all claims:
- Therapist's invoice with session date and duration
- Therapist's license number and credentials
- Diagnosis summary (confidentiality protected; submitted directly by provider)

**CL-064:** Pre-authorization form required for sessions beyond 8th session in a calendar year.

---

## 6. Claim Timeline

**CL-065:** Claims submitted quarterly or after completing a treatment block (minimum 4 sessions).

**CL-066:** Reimbursement within 14 business days; all claims handled with strict confidentiality protocols.

**CL-067:** Late claims accepted up to 60 days with manager's written waiver; this benefit-specific timeline overrides the general 30-day claim window (see CL-092).

---

## 7. Confidentiality & Privacy

**CL-068:** All mental health claims bypass standard HR review and go directly to third-party administrator.

**CL-069:** Claim history visible only to employee and benefits administrator; not accessible by line managers.

---

## Notes

**CL-070:** EAP services (available to all employees) do not count toward session limits or annual maximums.

**CL-071:** Crisis hotline services available 24/7 at no cost to all employees and dependents.
